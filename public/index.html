Architecture

Two-layer detection, built for the real world

A pure LLM call is too slow and too fragile for a live phone call. RakshaAI pairs an instant offline pattern engine with an LLM reasoning layer for semantic, never-seen-before scripts.

01

Capture

Live call audio (via telecom/OEM accessibility APIs) or pasted SMS/WhatsApp text is transcribed on-device in near real time.

02

Fast Heuristic Pass

A <50ms offline pattern engine scores known scam signatures — impersonation phrases, urgency, secrecy, OTP/fund-transfer requests — even with no network.

03

LLM Reasoning Layer

Claude analyzes full conversational context and intent to catch novel, paraphrased, or multilingual scam scripts the heuristic engine misses.

04

Act

Above threshold: on-screen warning in the caller's language, call-recording prompt, and one-tap alert to a pre-set family guardian with live risk context.

Why This Wins

Built to survive judging, not just impress a demo

Real, current, provable problem

Not a hypothetical — ₹22,495 crore lost in 2025 alone, with digital-arrest scams growing fastest. Every judge's parent or grandparent is the target user.

AI fighting AI

Scammers now use voice cloning and AI-generated scripts. RakshaAI is explicitly AI-vs-AI — a sharper, more defensible narrative than "AI for X."

Works without the internet

The offline heuristic layer keeps protecting users through poor rural connectivity — critical since first-time digital users in Tier-2/3 India are now the fastest-growing target group.

B2B2C revenue path

Licensable to banks (fraud-prevention mandate under RBI), telecoms (caller-risk layer), and insurers — not just a consumer app with no business model.

Designed for the actual victims

Elderly and first-time digital users, not developers — large text, voice-first alerts, regional language support, and a family safety net instead of relying on the victim alone.

Ships in 24 hours

The detection core, dashboard, and scanner you're using right now are fully working — not a mockup. Judges can test it live.
